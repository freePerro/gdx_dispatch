## Highlights
- An account is locked for 15 minutes after 10 wrong passwords since its last successful sign-in. The Users page shows which accounts are locked, and an admin can unlock one from there.
- On a tech's phone, finished jobs now show as finished.
- The schedule board and the open-slot finder now account for approved time off, so a tech who is off is no longer shown as free.
- Moving a scheduled job onto a tech past the end of their shift now asks first, both on the dispatch board and when booking from the phone.
- Vacation can now be counted by calendar year or from each person's hire date (anyone without a hire date stays on the calendar year). Set this in Settings, and add hire dates on the Users page. Every vacation balance shows which dates it covers.

## Changes you may notice
- The job and customer pickers on appointments search every job and customer, not just the first page.
- When a closeout books a second day, that visit is no longer labelled a callback.
- Estimate previews, the email composer and emailed estimate PDFs show the same expiry date the send sets.
- The dispatch drawer stays open when the board refreshes, and its times are shown in the shop's timezone.
- Pop-up messages no longer appear twice on several pages.
- The office inbox works on a phone-sized screen.
- On the phone, the day summary can be opened from the Today screen, and the Job Details buttons stay inside the card.
- Re-opening a finished job no longer leaves it marked done.
- Delete now works for custom fields, survey templates and tech phone tags.
- The survey list shows respondents and the last link sent.
- Status colors on the variance report and the customer portal match each status.
- An invoice in a locked accounting period can no longer be voided.
- Win-back texts are now actually sent, and a campaign is only marked sent once they go out.
- Several buttons and labels are easier to read in dark mode.
- PDF template editor tabs that had no effect were removed.

## Under the hood
- Unused tables and user columns are removed (see the warning above).
- Actions the AI assistant cannot perform are no longer reported as done, and its invoice void goes through the same checks as the Void button.
- More follow-up and reminder changes are written to the audit log.
- An old phone endpoint that marked a job done without completing it was removed.
