## Highlights
- What's new: this page. Click the version under the sidebar (or open More on a phone) to read what changed in each release, including older ones. After an update, a banner announces the new notes once per person.
- Sales tax now follows the job everywhere. Parts on repair and service-call jobs are taxed; installations are never taxed; tax-exempt customers are never taxed. Closeout drafts, office-created invoices, change orders and estimate-linked invoices all use the same rule.
- Every job gets a job number. Service calls, follow-ups and converted estimates are numbered when they are created, and older jobs without a number are numbered by this update.
- Vacation balances: each person's allowance, used and remaining days show on Timesheets, the Timeclock, the mobile Time off screen, the timesheet PDF and the CSV export.

## Changes you may notice
- Mobile closeout: the hours field now starts blank and has to be filled in. It no longer pre-fills from a running timer.
- A closeout invoice can only be made once the job is completed, and the last day's hours are not billed a second time on a later invoice.
- Customer pay page: "Use a different card" cancels the first card cleanly, and a bank micro-deposit wait can be released by the customer or the office.
- Customer portal: invoices and estimates refresh when you return to the tab, the tabs are easier to read on a phone, and sign-in errors say what went wrong.
- Send Reset Link and Send invite now actually email the link, or tell you why they could not.
- Estimates: a quantity over 9,999 is stopped at the field instead of failing on save, and an estimate with no tax rate warns you instead of quietly using 0%.

## Under the hood
- Migrations 117–120 run during the update. They fill in missing tax rates on open contractor and wholesale estimates, add a link from closeouts to the invoice that billed them, number jobs that had no number, and mark completed jobs as dispatch-done. Take a backup before updating.
- Payments and webhooks no longer stall for seconds when the background queue is down.
- Customer lifetime value now subtracts credit memos and skips void invoices.
