## Highlights
- Dropping a job on a tech's day on the dispatch board now checks whether the job's hours fit what is left of that tech's shift. When they do not, you can add a second tech, carry the rest over to the tech's next workdays, or keep it all on one day. A job that fits is scheduled the same way as before.
- Visits are now booked for the job's own hours. Before, an 8-hour job could be booked as a single 21-hour visit.
- The cost breakdown on a job now shows the labor variance.

## Changes you may notice
- The "Mobile field view (preview)" button in the top bar now works as a toggle. Click it again to go back to the desktop page you were on.
- Technicians are now linked to a real user login. When you set up a technician, you pick their login from a list.
- When someone is taken off a job's crew, they lose access to that job's chat, quote and day summary on the phone.
- An invoice a tech sends from the truck now records which tech sent it.
- During an outage you get one error message instead of one for every screen that keeps retrying.
- Signing out after being idle now takes you back to the page you were on when you sign in again.
- The email and lock icons on the sign-in page no longer cover the text on a phone.
- Cards on the dispatch timeline stay inside their lane.
- A shop timezone that is not a real timezone name is now refused when settings are saved.

## Under the hood
- More actions are now written to the audit log, including role changes (with the old role), user edits, crew changes (who was added and who was removed), inventory edits, vehicle inspections, diagnoses, hazards, receipts, and session and payroll settings.
- Assigning or removing a role now runs the same owner-only and permission checks everywhere, and assigning a new role replaces the old one instead of adding a second.
- Rate limits and audit entries now record the real client address, not the proxy's.
- An open dispatch board connection checks the sign-in again, so a deactivated user stops getting live updates.
- Several unused screens and connections were removed (an unused payroll summary and export, an old dispatch socket, and dead data-privacy code).
