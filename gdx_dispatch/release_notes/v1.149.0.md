## Highlights
- Holding lanes on dispatch now sit in one strip above the tech columns, on both the day and week boards, so you can drag a job between lanes or onto a tech without scrolling. Every job card has a Move to… menu, and a move can be undone.
- Dropping or assigning a job that has no date on a tech now asks how many hours it needs and who is on the crew.
- A new "Doors ordered" button on an install job marks all of its doors as ordered and moves the job to Waiting on doors in one step.
- Attach files to email: inbox mail (new, reply and forward), and the estimate, invoice and statement emails, can carry photos, job and customer documents, and estimate or invoice PDFs, on desktop and on the phone.
- On-my-way texts: when a tech taps "On my way" on the phone, or the office sends one, the customer can get a text naming the tech. It is off until an admin turns it on under Settings → Integrations → Phone.com.

## Changes you may notice
- Customers signed in to the customer portal are signed out once after this update and sign back in with their link or password.
- The job page's Time Entries card lets the office add, edit and delete hours the office entered. Hours a tech clocked stay locked.
- Day or Week on the dispatch board is remembered when you reload. A job dropped on "To schedule" comes off the schedule, and a job put in a tech's day leaves its holding lane.
- Moving a job on the board no longer drops part of an In Progress job's crew, and a finished job can no longer be dragged back onto the schedule.
- Invoice PDFs show the job number, the description of work and the payment terms, and estimate PDFs show the job number. The jobsite is no longer repeated when the billing address is a shorter form of it. Money on the pay page and the install sheet shows thousands separators.
- A tech's phone will not send the same invoice or receipt twice if Send is tapped again within about ten minutes.
- Inactive techs no longer appear as people you can assign on the board.

## Under the hood
- One database migration adds the on-my-way text setting (off by default). Nothing existing is changed.
- Sending or changing an estimate now needs the estimates.send or estimates.write permission.
- A one-time GL posting (opening balance, credit memo, refund, credit applied) is no longer posted twice when an invoice is recalculated. An admin repair tool (dry run by default) can find and reverse duplicates already in the books.
- Security fixes for portal sign-in tokens, plugin browser tickets, saved-request replay after a sign-out, and plugin catalog downloads.
