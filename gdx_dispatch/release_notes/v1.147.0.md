## Highlights
- Planner reminders: a planner task can now carry a reminder time. When it comes due, the person who set it gets a notification in the app. The GDX Planner phone app, which shows these reminders on an Android phone, is built and will be handed out separately.
- Finished jobs leave the dispatch board. A completed job shows only on the day it was completed, and visits left open on older completed jobs are closed, so the board, calendar and appointments list stop showing finished work as still to do.
- Receivables tie-out: the Ledger now checks that open invoice balances add up to the receivables account, and lists any invoice that does not. Until the bank tie-out is finished, the Ledger reports carry a "Provisional" banner.
- Customer tags can be used: filter the customer list by tag, see tags in a column, and build a segment of customers who have (or lack) a tag.

## Changes you may notice
- Re-doing the closeout of a job that is still completed now corrects the hours instead of adding new ones. If the job was billed for 2 hours and the closeout is corrected to 3, the next invoice offers 1 hour, not 3.
- If a corrected closeout comes in below what was already invoiced, the job names the earlier invoice to credit and fills in the amount.
- A closeout draft invoice that has not been sent or paid no longer marks the job as invoiced.
- A change order keeps the tax rate it was quoted and signed at, using the same job rules as invoices, so a repair quoted with no tax is no longer signed with tax the invoice never charges.
- A payment recorded against a void invoice becomes customer credit instead of reducing receivables below zero.
- Inviting a user with the Sales role works, and the Viewer role can be assigned.
- Adding, editing or removing technicians needs the permission to manage users.
- Integration cards in Settings (QuickBooks, bank feeds, email, phone) say "Status unknown" with a Retry button when the status cannot be loaded, instead of claiming they are not connected. The QuickBooks page keeps its sync log when the status check fails.

## Under the hood
- Migrations 124–127 run during the update. They add a stored tax rate to change orders, reminder fields to planner tasks, and correction fields to closeouts, none of which changes existing rows. Migration 126 closes the open visits of jobs completed before October 5. Take a backup before updating.
- Signing out now also cancels the access token in use, not only the refresh token, and the live dispatch board refuses a cancelled token and anyone who may not read the board.
- The security Redis now keeps its data on disk and never evicts it under memory pressure. It uses a new volume in all three compose files. Started empty, it forgets which signed-out sessions were revoked, so those stay usable until they expire (up to 7 days) for anyone already holding a copy.
- Groundwork for a quick PIN lock in the phone app. Nothing changes until it is switched on.
