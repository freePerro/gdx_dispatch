## Highlights
- When a tech closes out a job on the phone, the hours and crew size are filled in from that day's "I'm here" check-ins. The tech checks the numbers, changes them if they are wrong, and submits. Nothing is filled in when there is no check-in, when the time looks too short or too long, or when a timer was stopped automatically at the end of the shift.
- Closing out an install sold from an accepted estimate no longer asks the tech to pick a labor type. They only need to add parts they used or change the hours.

## Changes you may notice
- Under the hours, the closeout says whose check-in the figure came from and the time it was taken. A crew size the tech already typed is never replaced.

## Under the hood
- The closeout's audit record keeps the hours and crew the phone suggested next to the figures the tech submitted, and whether the tech took the suggestion, changed it, or typed their own. Billing still uses only the submitted figures. No database migration.
