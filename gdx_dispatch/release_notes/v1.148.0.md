## Highlights
- Photos in texts: attach a photo to a text from the office Messages page (pick, paste or drop it in) or from the tech's phone (camera or gallery). Each photo goes out as its own text, and the words you typed go with the first one. Photos are resized and turned upright automatically, and their location data is removed.
- Week board on dispatch: the week view now shows techs down the side and days across the top, with each day's booked hours against what that tech can work. Drag a job into any tech's day. The arrows move a whole week at a time.
- Crews stay together: moving, reassigning or unassigning a job with two or more techs no longer silently drops part of the crew. Dropping it on someone outside the crew asks whether to swap one tech for them or add them. Taking every tech off a job asks first, on the dispatch board, the job page and the phone.
- Estimate and invoice PDFs now show the job name. Invoice PDFs also show the jobsite when it differs from the billing address, a pay-online link with the card-fee notice, and whether the invoice is paid, partly paid, unpaid or past due.

## Changes you may notice
- An invoice emailed from a tech's phone, or through your own mail program when no email account is connected, now attaches a PDF that shows it as sent, with the pay link. Before, it could read "Payment Status: Draft" with no link.
- When your login expires, the app goes to the login page once and says why, instead of filling the screen with error popups.
- A text that may have gone through but was not confirmed now says so ("check the conversation before sending again"), instead of reading as failed.
- The Ledger's receivables tie-out shows the largest 200 shortfall payments and says how many there are in total.

## Under the hood
- No database migrations in this release.
- Every text and photo sent through Phone.com now leaves an audit record of who sent it, to which number (last 4 digits), and for which customer or job.
